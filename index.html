Our history

From one outreach, to a movement.

Make‑A‑Child‑Smile Foundation was founded in 2019 by Dave Lumor and Kwaku Kissi Obeng, out of a simple desire: to put smiles on the faces of children who don’t share the privileges many others do across Ghana. The Foundation’s first outreach, Charitas, marked the start of a journey that has since grown into a movement of kindness, empowerment, and impact.

That journey now includes Mentorship 101, Feed a Child This Christmas, and And That’s On Periodt! — each responding to a different need young people face, from guidance and belonging to nutrition and dignity. Since 2019, MACSF has reached over 850 beneficiaries, one child, one family, and one community at a time — with bigger dreams still ahead.
