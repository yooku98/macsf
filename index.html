Our mission

A generation of young people, showing up for the ones who need it most.

“To bring together a generation of like‑minded youth, who will work together to make life easier for the vulnerable children in society.”

Service

Showing up for children directly, not funding from a distance.

Impact

Measuring what actually changes for the children we work with.

Accountability

Owning outcomes to the communities and partners we serve.

Transparency

Open about where support goes and what it does once it arrives.

Consistency

A long‑term presence in a child's life, not a one‑off visit.

What we run

Four programs, one goal.

Flagship
Program · 01

Mentorship 101

Volunteers and guest speakers sit down with students in schools across Accra for candid talks on ambition, character, and getting through hard seasons — capped off with keepsake encouragement cards for every student in the room.

Program · 02

Feed A Child

Nutrition support for children who would otherwise go without a meal.

Program · 03

ATOP

An ongoing MACSF initiative supporting vulnerable children in local communities.

Program · 04

Charitas 2019
